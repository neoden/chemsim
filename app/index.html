About the model

The electrons are computed with the GFN2-xTB method right in the browser. What you see are the valence electrons: the inner shells are not part of this method.

What the model cannot do

  • The nuclei are classical. There is no zero-point motion, and at low temperatures the amplitudes are too small.
  • Dissociation at realistic temperatures is too rare to wait for. Heating to thousands of kelvin is a way to speed the event up.
  • The method is fitted to geometries, frequencies and intermolecular interactions. Reaction energies and barrier heights are approximate.
  • Light in a model like this can only be a classical wave. There is no spontaneous emission.
